\# Research Scope



This package is a candidate research input for Ohio record relief. It is not production law, legal advice, a court determination, an approved FairPath rule set, or authority to publish member-facing eligibility outcomes.



This package covers the Ohio mechanisms researched in the following areas:



\- Adult conviction sealing.

\- Adult conviction expungement.

\- Misdemeanor bail-forfeiture sealing and expungement.

\- Sealing and expungement after not guilty findings, dismissals, grand-jury no bills, and pardons.

\- Firearm-law-change expungement.

\- Human-trafficking-related conviction expungement.

\- Human-trafficking-related nonconviction expungement.

\- Prosecutor-initiated low-level controlled-substance sealing or expungement.

\- Juvenile sealing and expungement.

\- Certificate of Qualification for Employment, abbreviated CQE.

\- Official forms and court-specific procedure.

\- Fees, waiver rules, filing courts, hearings, prosecutor notice, and source provenance.

\- Change monitoring and source-staleness controls.



\# Candidate Research Principles



\- Ohio Revised Code text published through Ohio Laws is the primary source for statewide eligibility and legal-effect rules.

\## Official Records



R.C. 2953.31 defines “official records” broadly, including records possessed by public offices or agencies that relate to a criminal case, as specified in the statute.



Primary authority:

\- Ohio Revised Code Section 2953.31(A)(3).



Official URL:

\- https://codes.ohio.gov/ohio-revised-code/section-2953.31



\## Record of Conviction



“Record of conviction” means the record related to a conviction or guilty plea to an offense.



Primary authority:

\- Ohio Revised Code Section 2953.31(A)(11).



Official URL:

\- https://codes.ohio.gov/ohio-revised-code/section-2953.31



\## Bail Forfeiture



A bail-forfeiture route exists under Ohio law for a specific type of agreed misdemeanor bail forfeiture. The actual underlying case disposition and agreement facts must be verified.

\## No Bill



A no bill is a grand-jury report that no indictment was found against a person held to answer.



Primary authority:

\- Ohio Revised Code Section 2953.31(A)(13).



Official URL:

\- https://codes.ohio.gov/ohio-revised-code/section-2953.31



\## Victim of Human Trafficking



A victim of human trafficking is a person who is or was victimized under R.C. 2905.32. The statutory definition does not require conviction of the trafficker.



Primary authority:

\- Ohio Revised Code Section 2953.31(A)(12).



Official URL:

\- https://codes.ohio.gov/ohio-revised-code/section-2953.31



\## Certificate of Qualification for Employment



A CQE is collateral-consequence relief. It does not seal or expunge a criminal record.



Primary authority:

\- Ohio Revised Code Section 2953.25.



Official URL:

\- https://codes.ohio.gov/ohio-revised-code/section-2953.25



\# Candidate Rule Classification Definitions



\## DETERMINISTIC



A date or rule component can be calculated from validated, legally correct, complete inputs.



Example:

\- Six months after a verified final-discharge event for a qualifying minor misdemeanor.



A deterministic date calculation does not establish legal eligibility or court outcome.



\## MEMBER\_ASSERTED\_FACT



FairPath may ask the member for the fact but cannot assume it is independently verified.



Examples:

\- Whether a criminal proceeding is pending.

\- Whether all sentence conditions have ended.

\- Whether the member has a CDL.

\- Whether a civil action remains pending.

\- Whether the member was subject to Chapter 2950 requirements.



\## CLASSIFICATION\_REQUIRED



An offense, disposition, document, court event, or relationship must be legally classified before applying the candidate rule.



Examples:

\- Exact statute and subsection.

\- Offense degree.

\- Similar municipal ordinance.

\- Felony offense of violence.

\- Sexually oriented offense.

\- Chapter 2950 status.

\- Victim-under-13 issue.

\- F3 count rule.

\- OVI or physical-control offense.

\- Same-act charge relationship.



\## MANUAL\_REVIEW\_REQUIRED



The engine must route the member or case for qualified review. The engine must not decide the legal issue.



Examples:

\- Final discharge unclear.

\- Dismissal without prejudice.

\- Human-trafficking nexus.

\- Historical firearm statute.

\- F3 count rule.

\- Same-act multiple-charge issue.

\- CQE barrier coverage.

\- Court-specific filing form unavailable.



\## COURT\_DISCRETION



The court must make an individualized finding, and satisfying objective elements does not guarantee relief.



Examples:

\- Rehabilitation.

\- Government-interest balancing.

\- Victim or prosecutor objections.

\- CQE public-safety/material-assistance standards.

\- Juvenile benefit/harm findings.

\- Certain related-conviction aggregation decisions.

\### Potentially Applies To



A person with a qualifying Ohio conviction or guilty plea, subject to exclusions, waiting periods, complete conviction-count analysis, pending-case status, and court findings.



For a qualifying federal or other-state conviction, the statute directs filing in an Ohio court of common pleas.



\### Waiting-Period Rules



\#### One or Two Eligible F3 Convictions



Waiting Period:

\- Three years.



Clock Start:

\- Final discharge.



Conditions:

\- One or two F3 convictions after application of the statutory count rules.

\- Neither subject conviction may be R.C. 2921.43.

\- No statutory exclusion may apply.

\- No pending criminal proceeding.

\- Court must find rehabilitation satisfactory.

\- Court must find the applicant’s interest is not outweighed by legitimate governmental need to maintain records.



Authority:

\- R.C. 2953.32(B)(1)(a)(i).

\- R.C. 2953.32(A)(1)(h).

\- R.C. 2953.32(A)(3).

\- R.C. 2953.32(D).



Automation Classification:

\- DETERMINISTIC for arithmetic only after verified category and final-discharge event.

\- MEMBER\_ASSERTED\_FACT for final discharge and pending proceedings.

\- CLASSIFICATION\_REQUIRED for offense degree, F3 count, exclusions, and related-conviction rules.

\- MANUAL\_REVIEW\_REQUIRED for F3 count and related-case analysis.

\- COURT\_DISCRETION for rehabilitation, balancing, and public-interest decisions.



Known Ambiguity:

\- “Final discharge” is used but not self-defined in the retrieved current R.C. 2953.32 text.

\- F3 count analysis may require full conviction history and related-case facts.

\- Convictions may be treated as one in certain related circumstances, but the court may decide that counting them as one would not be in the public interest.

\- No subject offense may be R.C. 2921.43.

\- No subject offense may be a felony offense of violence.

\- No R.C. 2953.32(A)(1) exclusion may apply.

\- No pending criminal proceeding.

\- Court must make rehabilitation and balancing findings.



Authority:

\- R.C. 2953.32(B)(1)(a)(ii).

\- R.C. 2953.32(A)(1).

\- R.C. 2953.32(D).
