# Employer Jobs Pricing — LOCKED

## Individual 30-day postings

| Product | Price | Notes |
|---|---:|---|
| First Job | $0 once | One free 30-day posting per verified company |
| Standard Job | $39 / 30 days | Easy Apply, screening questions, applicant inbox, statuses, website link, basic analytics |
| Featured Job | $69 / 30 days | Standard + boosted placement + Featured badge + alert/home exposure |
| Priority Hire | $99 / 30 days | Featured + strongest placement + matched-candidate suggestions + limited outreach value |

## Employer subscriptions

| Plan | Price | Capacity |
|---|---:|---|
| Starter | $79/mo | 3 active job slots |
| Growth | $179/mo | 10 active job slots + 100 candidate contacts/month + team/analytics expansion |
| Pro | $349/mo | 25 active job slots + 300 candidate contacts/month + automation + advanced analytics + incentive matching |
| Enterprise | $799+/mo | High-volume/custom active slots, multi-location, integrations, reporting/support |

Subscription slots are reusable. Closing/filling a job immediately frees the slot.

## Candidate sourcing packs

| Pack | Price |
|---|---:|
| 25 candidate contacts | $29 |
| 100 candidate contacts | $79 |
| 250 candidate contacts | $149 |

Browsing/matching can be shown without charge. A contact credit is consumed when the employer proactively invites/messages a candidate who has not applied.

## Boosts

- 7-day boost: $15
- 30-day Featured upgrade: +$30
- Priority upgrade from Standard: +$60

## Annual pricing direction

Use approximately two months free:
- Starter: $790/year
- Growth: $1,790/year
- Pro: $3,490/year

## Job lifecycle

- Standard job term: 30 days
- Employer may close earlier
- Filled job can be marked Filled
- Expired jobs stop accepting applications and disappear from active search
- Renewal creates another 30-day term
- Subscription customers reuse freed slots

## Pricing principles

- Never charge the applicant to apply.
- Never charge employers per job view.
- Never charge employers to view people who applied to their own job.
- Easy Apply is part of the posting value, not a per-application fee.
- Monetize proactive sourcing, premium placement, recruiting capacity, automation, incentive assistance, enterprise tools, and future protection products.
- First free job is for verified companies and is intended as acquisition, not an unlimited free tier.

# Housing Provider Pricing — WORKSHOP

## Product principle

Housing should undercut the large rental platforms on paid provider features while keeping basic participation easy. FairPath's differentiator is not generic property-management software. It is justice-impacted housing access, reusable renter profiles, FastTrack applications, landlord incentive matching, future FairPath Housing Protection, and a qualified renter pool.

Do not try to beat free landlord software by charging landlords for basic tools those platforms give away. Charge for FairPath-specific value.

## Recommended launch structure

### First listing
**$0 once / 30 days per verified housing provider**

Includes:
- one active listing
- FairPath renter discovery
- standard inquiry/application flow
- basic applicant inbox
- housing-policy fields
- property website/company link
- basic listing analytics

### Standard listing
**$19 / 30 days**

Includes:
- full listing
- applicant inbox
- reusable FairPath renter profile intake
- housing-policy details
- transparent fee/rent fields
- screening-question configuration
- standard analytics

### Featured listing
**$39 / 30 days**

Includes Standard plus:
- boosted search placement
- Featured badge
- relevant renter alerts
- additional home/housing discovery exposure

### Priority Lease
**$69 / 30 days**

Includes Featured plus:
- strongest FairPath placement
- qualified-renter match suggestions
- limited proactive renter invitations
- landlord incentive-match preview when available

This stays close enough to the old Friend A Felon concept ($14.99 basic / $24.99 featured) to preserve the original low-friction intent, but gives FairPath room to build a real company.

## Provider subscriptions

### Starter Property
**$49/month**
- 5 active listing slots
- core applicant management
- standard FairPath renter discovery

### Growth Property
**$99/month**
- 20 active listing slots
- team access
- 100 proactive renter invites/month
- stronger analytics
- landlord incentive matching

### Portfolio
**$199/month**
- 75 active listing slots
- 300 proactive renter invites/month
- advanced analytics
- multi-property workflows
- priority incentive tools
- future FastTrack/provider automation

### Enterprise / Multifamily
**$499+/month custom**
- high-volume listings
- multiple properties/teams
- integrations/API
- bulk import
- custom reporting
- institutional/portfolio workflows

Slots are reusable when a unit is rented, withdrawn, or closed.

## Renter applications

### Standard FairPath housing application
**Free to submit**

Do not charge the renter simply to express interest or submit FairPath profile/application data.

### Third-party screening
Credit/background/eviction reports may have a separate pass-through fee when actually ordered. FairPath should show the fee before consent and obey state/local application-fee laws.

### FastTrack Housing — preserve existing concept
Existing product direction:
- $75/application standard
- $65/application with FairPath+ ($2/month)

FastTrack must be materially different from a normal application. It should eventually include a premium package such as:
- verified identity/profile
- income/document verification
- reusable application packet
- landlord-facing completeness check
- application delivery/status tracking
- incentive/protection eligibility check
- structured denial/reason workflow where appropriate
- future FairPath Housing Protection eligibility review

Do not make every housing application a $75 FastTrack application.

## Proactive renter sourcing

Housing providers can browse anonymized qualified matches and spend credits only to proactively invite a renter who has not applied.

Proposed add-ons:
- 25 renter invites: $19
- 100 renter invites: $59
- 250 renter invites: $119

Do not expose private phone/email/address simply because a provider bought credits. Initial outreach stays inside FairPath.

## Housing boosts

- 7-day boost: $10
- Featured upgrade for remaining term: +$20
- Priority Lease upgrade from Standard: +$50

## Landlord incentive tools

- Basic program-match visibility: include in Growth and above
- Assisted incentive filing: separate paid service later
- FairPath Housing Protection: separate product economics; do not bundle regulated protection cost into listing price

## Housing lifecycle direction

Recommended listing term: 30 days, renewable.

A rented unit immediately closes and frees a subscription slot.

Possible statuses:
- draft
- published
- paused
- pending lease
- rented
- withdrawn
- expired

## Why this model

Current rental software competitors commonly give landlords basic listings/applications for free or very cheaply, so FairPath should not rely on generic listing software as the main paid value.

FairPath's paid value is:
- justice-impacted renter access
- verified/reusable renter readiness
- FastTrack
- proactive matching
- landlord incentives
- future FairPath Housing Protection
- higher-intent applicants
- portfolio workflows
