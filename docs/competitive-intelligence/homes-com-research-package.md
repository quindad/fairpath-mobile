# Competitive Intelligence Research Package: Homes.com
**Researcher:** Juice | **Date:** 2026-10-09 | **Method:** Claude Browser pane, public pages only.

## 1. Overview
Homes.com is owned by **CoStar Group**, which also owns **Apartments.com, ApartmentFinder.com, ApartmentHomeLiving.com, ForRent.com, LoopNet.com, Land.com, NewHomeSource.com, and Domain.com.au** (full sibling-brand list observed in the site footer). This is a major structural finding for the research plan: Homes.com and Apartments.com are **not independent competitors** — they're the same company's two consumer-facing brands, likely sharing backend listing data and infrastructure. Research on one should inform, not duplicate, research on the other.

## 2. Feature inventory **[OBSERVED]**
- **AI Advisor** — a visible "Open AI Advisor" button directly in the search results toolbar (not explored further this pass — button exists, contents unverified).
- **National Housing Market report** on the homepage: live-updating stats (homes for sale count, days on market, median sale price, month-over-month deltas) — a data/content feature, not interactive.
- **Cost Calculator** per listing — estimates total monthly + one-time move-in costs beyond base rent, built into the listing page itself.
- **Full, itemized fee disclosure** per listing: application fee ($55 observed), non-refundable admin fee ($150 observed), explicitly labeled "Due at Application." This is a real, structured fee-transparency feature — base rent is clearly separated from required one-time fees.
- **Matterport 3D tours** integrated directly into listings (observed as a listed feature, not test-clicked).
- Floor-plan browser (filter by bed count, view per-unit pricing within one property listing).
- "Send Message" / "Schedule Tour" as the two primary listing CTAs — no in-platform application/screening flow observed (tour-request and inquiry, not an apply-online flow, at least on this listing).
- Detailed amenity taxonomy (property-level and unit-level amenities shown separately).
- **No background-check/screening-criteria disclosure observed anywhere on the listing** — unlike FairPath's housing detail screen, which already explicitly discloses screening policy ("Credit and rental history required. Background check reviewed against a 7-year lookback for certain offense categories," confirmed live earlier this engagement). This is a genuine, already-existing FairPath advantage worth naming plainly.

## 3. FairPath comparison

| Homes.com capability | FairPath | Classification |
|---|---|---|
| Itemized move-in cost calculator | FairPath's housing detail already shows a "Cost Snapshot" (monthly rent, deposit, application fee, estimated listed upfront total — confirmed live earlier this engagement) | **Near parity** — FairPath already does something structurally similar; worth a closer side-by-side later, not an urgent gap. |
| Screening-policy transparency on listing | **FairPath already discloses this per-listing**; Homes.com does not (at least on the listing observed) | **FairPath advantage** — directly relevant to FairPath's core audience in a way the generalist competitor doesn't address at all. |

## 4. Recommendation
No urgent action — FairPath's existing housing detail screen already compares favorably on the dimension that matters most for this audience (screening transparency). The cost-calculator and 3D-tour gaps are nice-to-haves, not differentiators competitors are winning on for FairPath's specific audience.
